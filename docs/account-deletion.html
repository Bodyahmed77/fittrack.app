Fifty Fit — Account & Data Deletion

This page lets you request deletion of your Fifty Fit account and associated app data if you no longer have the app installed.

Submit the request from the email address used for your Fifty Fit account. We may ask for additional information to verify account ownership before completing the deletion request. Google Play subscriptions are managed separately and are not cancelled automatically by deleting a Fifty Fit account.

The form opens an email request addressed to Fifty Fit support. If your email app does not open, contact sumoslmer47@gmail.com directly from the email address used for your Fifty Fit account.
