Fifty Fit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Fifty Fit provides fitness tracking, workout planning, nutrition tracking and an optional AI Coach. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, Google sign-in information, and a phone number required for account support and custom-plan communication.
  • Fitness information you enter, including age, gender, height, weight, goals and activity information.
  • Workout history, weight logs and nutrition entries.
  • Subscription and entitlement status needed to provide paid features.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the app.

AI Coach

When you use AI Coach, the app sends the information needed to answer your request to Google's Gemini API. Fifty Fit does not maintain a chat-history database; chat messages are held in app memory while the AI Coach screen is open. If you report an AI answer, the reported answer snippet and your report reason are stored in Supabase for safety and quality review. Google's processing and retention are governed by the applicable Gemini API terms and policies.

How data is stored and shared

Account and app data use Google Firebase services. Subscription entitlement and AI usage data use Supabase. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing; Fifty Fit does not receive or store your payment card details.

We do not sell personal information.

Account and data deletion

You can start account deletion from the app's Settings → Delete Account screen. We remove the Firebase account and Firestore profile data and delete the associated Fifty Fit server-side records held in Supabase, including entitlement, purchase-token-claim and AI-usage records.

Google Play subscription cancellation is separate from account deletion. Deleting a Fifty Fit account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it from Google Play's subscription center before or after requesting account deletion.

If you no longer have the app installed, use the external deletion request page:

Request Fifty Fit account/data deletion

Children

Fifty Fit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
